# Wessex Translational Genomic Informatics Collaborative (WeTGI)

## About Us

The **Wessex Translational Genomic Informatics Collaborative (WTGIC)** is a partnership bringing together expertise from academia, healthcare, and advanced computing infrastructure to accelerate the translation of genomic research into clinical practice.

### Partners

- **University of Southampton**
  - Genomic Informatics
  - Bioinformatics Research Facility (Bio-R)
  - IRIDIS High Performance Computing Facility

- **NHS Genomic Medicine Service**
  - Wessex Genomic Laboratory Services

- **University Hospital Southampton**
  - Southampton Emerging Technologies and Therapeutics

- **NIHR Southampton Biomedical Research Centre**
  - Data, Health & Society

- **Wessex Secure Data Environment**

"The Wessex Translational Genomic Informatics Collaborative creates a cross-institutional team comprising NHS clinical bioinformaticians and data scientists alongside their academic counterparts from the University of Southampton, bolstered by the infrastructure enabled through NHS secure data environments and academic supercompute facilities. Through close collaboration combining practical experience of NHS genomic medicine with research expertise in computational biology, software development and genomic informatics - we demonstrate synergy and advance real-world clinical translational value of genomic medicine"
